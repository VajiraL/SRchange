# Technical Summary: $S_R$ Modeling & Scenario Pipeline

## 1. Modeling Framework
- **Algorithm:** XGBoost Regression with CUDA acceleration.
- **Objective Function:** `count:poisson` (optimized for storage capacity distributions).
- **Validation:** 3-ensemble, 5-fold spatial block cross-validation (15 models total).
- **Clustering Strategy:** Climate-cluster guided modeling ($k=6$). Separate models are trained for 6 distinct climate zones to capture regional sensitivities.
- **Accuracy:** $R^2 = 0.755$, MAE = 48.1 mm, KGE = 0.796 (Weighted test split).

## 2. Feature Engineering
- **Terrain (3):** Elevation, Slope, Roughness.
- **Climate (40):** Annual/Seasonal indices (precip, temp, aridity, PET) and percentile-based variability metrics.
- **Land Use (4):** Global Human Modification (gHM), Irrigation Fraction, Rainfed Fraction, and Forest Age.
- **Consistency:** Training utilizes synthetic 1991–2020 LU features to ensure statistical consistency with scenario predictions.

## 3. Scenario Matrix (168 Combinations)
Predictions are generated by crossing 12 Climate periods with 14 Land Use (LU) configurations.

### Climate Dimensions (12)
- **Historical:** 1901–1930, 1931–1960, 1961–1990, 1991–2020.
- **Future:** 2041–2070 and 2071–2099 for SSP126, SSP245, SSP370, and SSP460.

### Land Use Dimensions (14)
- **Idealized:** `Intact` (No human modification), `PI` (Pre-industrial ~1700-1850).
- **Historical:** Mode LU for periods 1901–1930 through 1991–2020.
- **Future:** SSP-consistent LU projections for 2041–2070 and 2071–2099.

## 4. Aggregation & Storage
- **Unified Zarr Store:** `output_SR/aggregated_sr_scenarios.zarr`
- **Structure:** 5D array with coordinates `[climate, lu, ensemble_stat, lat, lon]`.
- **Ensemble Stats:** Mean and Standard Deviation (of the 15 models) for both Global ($k=0$) and Clustered ($k=6$) models.
- **Training Bias Correction:** The 1991–2020 Standard scenario uses only test-fold predictions to ensure the aggregated map remains an independent assessment of model performance.

## 5. Directory Structure
- `data/LU_features/`: Synthetic land use predictor sets.
- `data/climate_features.zarr`: Multi-period climate forcing.
- `output_SR/scenario_predictions/`: Individual ensemble-fold NetCDF outputs.
- `output_SR/aggregated_sr_scenarios.zarr`: Final analysis-ready dataset.
